About Tea Time Cafe

Tea Time Cafe started as a small counter with two espresso machines and a big idea: coffee tastes better when it's made by people who care about the details. Today we still roast in small batches, bake our pastries fresh every morning, and greet every regular by name. Come in, take a seat, and stay a while.
